## Methodology

### Time-Series Cross-Validation

To prevent data leakage and ensure academic rigor, we implemented **walk-forward validation**:

1. **Initial Training Period:** 365 days
2. **Forecast Horizon:** 30 days
3. **Step Size:** 30 days (moving forward between folds)
4. **Number of Folds:** 5

This approach ensures:
- ✅ No future data leaks into training
- ✅ Realistic evaluation of production performance
- ✅ Robust metrics with confidence intervals

## Interpretation Guide

### What do these metrics mean?

- **MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error):** Average percentage difference between predicted and actual values. Lower is better. <10% is excellent, 10-20% is good, >20% needs improvement.

- **RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error):** Average magnitude of errors in rupees. Penalizes large errors more heavily. Lower is better.

- **MAE (Mean Absolute Error):** Average absolute error in rupees. More interpretable than RMSE. Lower is better.

- **R² (Coefficient of Determination):** Proportion of variance explained by the model. Ranges from 0 to 1. Higher is better. >0.8 is good, >0.9 is excellent.
