# Entry template

Copy this block to the top of the relevant section in `01-timeline.md`, and update the topic file (`02`–`10`) as well.
Write the entry before the commit.

---

## YYYY-MM-DD — short title

**Request (as given):** the owner's words, verbatim where possible.

**What changed**
- File or page: `path/to/file` — function, component or table changed, and how.
- …

**Why this approach**
- The problem being solved, in one or two sentences.
- The reasoning behind the chosen approach, step by step, including the evidence that led to it (a measurement, a failing test, a user report).

**Alternatives considered**
- Option A: why it was not chosen.
- Option B: why it was not chosen.

**Trade-offs and risks**
- What this costs (speed, complexity, memory, maintenance).
- What could go wrong, and how it would show.

**Decision record**
- New decision? Add it to `02-decisions.md` as `D<next number>`.
- Source: *recorded* (where) or *reconstructed*.

**Verification (as run)**
- Command or test, and its result. Failures included, with what was done about them.
- Screens checked, if the change is visible.

**Follow-ups**
- Anything left open. Add it to `11-open-items.md` as well.
