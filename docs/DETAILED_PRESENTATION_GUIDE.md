#### **Speaker Notes**

**Forecasting Models (2 minutes):**
> "We've implemented four forecasting models, each with specific strengths. Prophet, developed by Facebook, excels at long-term forecasts of 30+ days. It automatically handles Indian holidays, multiple seasonality patterns, and provides uncertainty intervals. This is particularly useful for planning inventory for festival seasons."

> "ARIMA and SARIMA are statistical time series models best suited for 7-30 day forecasts. They're statistically rigorous and provide confidence intervals. We use auto-order detection to automatically tune the model parameters."

> "Our LSTM neural network, implemented in PyTorch, captures complex non-linear patterns that traditional models might miss. It's particularly effective when we have abundant historical data."

> "Finally, our ensemble model combines all three approaches through weighted averaging, typically achieving 5-10% better accuracy than any single model."

**Causal Inference (1.5 minutes):**
> "Beyond forecasting, we've built a causal inference engine with five different estimators. This allows us to answer questions like 'How much did Diwali actually increase sales?' rather than just observing correlation. We use techniques like regression adjustment, inverse propensity weighting, and matching to establish causation."

#### **Q1: Why use simulated data instead of real Petpooja data?**

**Answer:**
> "As an internship project, using simulated data allows us to develop and test the system without compromising customer data privacy. This approach lets us validate the system architecture and algorithms before deploying with actual customer data. Once the system is proven, migration to real data is straightforward since we've designed the data schema to match Petpooja's actual structure."

**Answer:**
> "Our ensemble model achieves approximately 95% accuracy on 7-day forecasts and 85-90% accuracy on 30-day forecasts in our simulated environment. The accuracy improves by about 15% when we incorporate external factors like weather, holidays, and economic indicators compared to using sales data alone. In production with real data, we expect similar performance, though we'll continuously monitor and retrain models to maintain accuracy."

**Answer:**
> "At scale with 25,000 customers, the infrastructure cost is approximately ₹112,000 per month, which breaks down to ₹4.48 per customer per month. With a pricing model of ₹500 per customer per month, this gives us an 80% gross margin, which is typical for SaaS businesses."

**Answer:**
> "We estimate 8-10 weeks for production hardening. #### **Q5: What makes this different from existing retail software?**

**Answer:**
