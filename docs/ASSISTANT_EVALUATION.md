## Limitations

- The questions are written by the developer; real users phrase things in more ways than the rule-based parser covers. Unrecognised questions fall back to a help message (or to the local LLM when installed).
- Checks verify that reported figures equal the database; they do not judge whether the explanation of a change is the true business cause (driver effects other than bills and average bill are estimates).
- Results are on synthetic data.
